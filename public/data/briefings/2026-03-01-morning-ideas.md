- Post-event engagement (they'll want their stats)

- Combines happen THIS weekend → urgency → quick yes/no

- Creates "proof of concept" — if 200 combine athletes sign up Sunday, Michael has validation narrative for Week 2 fundraising
- Low friction (free) = high conversion

**Execution:** 
- Find 10-15 AU/Asia youth basketball combines this weekend (Google "AAU combine" + dates)

**Expected outcome:** 50-150 high-intent signups over 48h, with 5-10 willing to create testimonial videos.

## IDEA 2: Founder Brand Play — "Coach's Truth" Podcast Teaser Series

**Angle:** Michael as the "anti-influencer" founder — using data to debunk myths coaches believe.

**Tactical Move:**
- **Format:** 2-3 minute audio snippets (TikTok/Reels length), not full podcast yet
- **Topic:** "Why your athletes are slower than you think (and what to do about it)"
  - Use CourtLab's real athlete speed data to show: most coaches overestimate athlete velocity
  - Show the gap between "what coaches see" vs "what CourtLab measures"
  - CTA: "Find out your athlete's real speed. Link in bio."
- **Distribution:** Post as Reels (IG/FB), TikToks, Shorts

**Draft Hook:**
```
"I measured 10,000 basketball athletes. Their coaches thought they were 'fast.'

Here's what the data actually showed... [reveal gap]

This is why coaches need data, not gut feel."
```

**Why it works:**
- Founder face + contrarian take = memorable, shareable
- Coaches WANT to believe in their athletes, but secretly want data to prove it
- Teaser series costs $0 (use voice, screen recordings, simple B-roll)
- Creates "founder authority" narrative that attracts creators + partners

**Execution:**
- Record 3x 2-min snippets this week (use existing CourtLab data, Michael's voice)
- Post to IG Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts (same video, 3 platforms)
- CTA to Lite version signup
- Track engagement (saves, shares, link clicks)

**Expected outcome:** 30-50 founder-aware signups, positioning Michael as data authority, social proof for future creator partnerships.

## IDEA 3: B2B Micro-Partnership — Youth Sports Equipment Retailers (Dick's Sporting Goods, Academy Sports)

**Angle:** CourtLab as the "athlete qualification tool" inside their stores — turn browser traffic into measurable athlete profiles.

**Tactical Move:**
- **Target:** Store managers / regional marketing leads at Dick's / Academy / local AU sports retailers
- **Pitch:** "Free athlete profiling booth at your store. Attracts parents, qualifies hot athletes, gives you data your competitors don't have."
- **Mechanics:** QR code at register → free 60-second CourtLab profile (vertical, speed, agility) → parent gets email with profile + coaching recommendations
- **Your upside:** Email list of high-intent parents, usage data, retail brand validation
- **Their upside:** Traffic driver, customer data, unique experience

**Draft Pitch Email:**
```
Subject: Free "Athlete Profile" Booth for Your Store

Hi [Manager Name],

We're testing a new model: free athlete profiling at sports retail locations. 

How it works:
- Parents bring athlete in, takes 90 seconds (vertical jump, sprint, agility)
- Kid gets instant profile + ranking
- Parent gets email with coaching tips

Free for you. You get foot traffic + customer insight.

Interested in piloting this at [Store Location]?
```

**Why it works:**
- Retailers want foot traffic + customer data (we solve both)
- Parents are in-store anyway, low friction to try
- Creates "retail validation" narrative (if Dick's pilots, credibility balloons)
- Generates parent email list (high buyer intent)

**Execution:**
- Identify 5-10 retailers within 30min of Michael or partner cities
- Call store manager directly (warmer than email)
- Propose 1-month pilot (free, zero risk for them)
- Ship simple hardware: iPad + tripod + marking tape

**Expected outcome:** 1-2 retail pilots, 100-300 qualified parent signups, case study content ("How we qualified 500 young athletes in 2 weeks").
