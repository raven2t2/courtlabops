## IDEA #1: "Benchmarked Talent" — Lead Magnet to Break Role-Selection Bottleneck

### The Problem
Users sign up, select role, then drop. They're not seeing value before trial gate. We need to deliver ONE "aha moment" that proves Combine's value before asking for commitment.

### The Move
**Free Lead Magnet: "Is Your Point Guard Combine-Ready?"** — Simple PDF assessment that coaches/parents can use offline. Includes:
- 8-point evaluation rubric (footwork, IQ, conditioning, decision-making, touch, strength, athleticism, basketball IQ)
- Benchmark data: "Elite U15 PGs: 65%+ FT, 6+ APG, handle pressure for 90 sec+" 
- "How You Compare" section (coaches self-score, get 1-10 ranking)
- **CTA:** "See how your full team stacks up on CourtLab Combine"

### Specific Tactical Execution

**Step 1: Create 3 Position PDFs This Week** (15 min each)
- PG Evaluation (most traffic)
- SG/Wing Evaluation 
- Big Evaluation

**Step 2: Deploy to 3 Traffic Sources**
- r/Basketball (post: "Free coach resource: PG evaluation rubric")
- r/Coaching (same)
- Basketball coaching Facebook groups (30+ groups, 50K+ coaches)

**Step 3: Email Capture**
- Gate PDF behind email → add to CRM nurture sequence
- Nurture: Day 1 (PDF), Day 3 (case study: "How Coach Mike went from no data to recruiting clarity"), Day 7 (Combine trial invite)

### Why It Works
- **Removes friction:** Users get value WITHOUT logging in. They experience Combine philosophy offline first.
- **Addresses pain #2:** Coaches explicitly asked "how do I objectively evaluate talent?" — this answers it.
- **Breaks the funnel:** They self-score offline, THEN realize they need full team data → trial friction drops.
- **Cheap to execute:** Haiku + templates = $0.50 content, infinite reach
- **Proof point:** If 100 PDFs download and 10 trial → we have a repeatable conversion wedge

**Expected Conversion:** 3-5% PDF → trial (low bar, but beats 0%)

## IDEA #3: "Combine Invitational" — In-Person Event + Recruitment Multiplier

### The Problem
CourtLab is digital/abstract. Coaches are skeptical of tools they haven't seen live. We need to prove Combine in person, build coaches' belief, and generate organic word-of-mouth.

### The Move
**Partner with 1 youth basketball tournament/AAU organizer** to run a live Combine event inside their tournament.

- Scoring: Live tablet scoring (coaches SEE data capture in real time)
- Output: Each player gets instant PDF report with benchmarks
- Multiplier: Coaches watching = free marketing (peer-to-peer credibility)

**Targets (Find This Week):**
- Easter Classic (Easter is April 20 — 8 weeks out, PERFECT timing)
- Local AAU tournament (next 4 weeks)
- Regional basketball combine event

**Partnership Structure:**

**Why It Works:**
- **Live proof:** Coaches SEE data being generated. Removes "is this real?" doubt.

- **Recruit the recruiters:** Coaches at tournament tell other coaches → organic funnel
