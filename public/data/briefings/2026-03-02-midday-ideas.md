## ⚡ QUICK-WIN TACTICAL MOVE #1: "Free Combine Calculator" MVP (Deploy by EOD)

### The Problem
CourtLab has 35 free users but **zero activated** (0 trials, 0 paid). Coaches are signing up but not trying the core product.

Real barrier: They don't know if their players will benefit until they see actual data. Current paywall comes too early.

### The Move
**Launch "Combine Calculator" micro-tool** (free, no signup):
- Coach enters: Player name, position, 5 key metrics (vertical jump, speed, 3pt%, ...)
- Instant output: 
  - "Your player is in top 25% for guards nationally"
  - "Comparison to AU recruits at same age"
  - "What scouts look for at this position" (educational)
  - **CTA: "See full player profile + recruiting insights →" (trial signup)**

**Why This Works:**
- **No friction:** Try before signup (proven 3-5x conversion uplift in SaaS)
- **Viral potential:** Coaches share calc with parents + players ("See where you rank")
- **Proof of concept:** Validates that coaches want this product
- **Lead magnet:** Capture email before offering trial
- **SEO win:** "Basketball player evaluator" + "combine calculator Australia" rank for organic trial traffic

### Implementation (2 hours, ready by 3 PM)

**1. Build MVP Calculator (1 hour)**
- Tech stack: React component (reuse existing CourtLab scoring logic)
- Input form: 5 dropdowns (position, metrics)
- Output: Simple comparison chart (your player vs. AU average vs. top 25%)
- Design: Mobile-first (coaches use on sideline)
- Deploy: courtlabops.vercel.app/calc (zero auth required)

- **Reddit (r/basketball AU):** "Hey, made a free tool for coach evaluation in Australia. Check it out → [link]"

## 📱 CONTENT ANGLE #2: TikTok Series — "Coach Mistakes" (1 post every 2 days)

### The Problem
CourtLab's TikTok gets 0 engagement because content is **product-focused** ("here's our app") not **coach-focused** ("here's what's hurting your recruiting").

Coaches don't follow product accounts. They follow accounts that teach them stuff.

### The Move
**Launch weekly "Common Coach Mistakes" series** on TikTok:
- 15-30 sec video (quick pattern interrupt)
- Each video: 1 recruiting mistake → data-backed fix
- Tone: Educational, slightly sarcastic, coach-to-coach
- Closes with: "Want your player data analyzed for free? CourtLab calc →"

### Why This Works
- **Engagement magnet:** Coaches share with their assistants ("This is exactly what we do")
- **Authority:** Shows CourtLab understands coach problems (not selling, teaching)
- **Organic reach:** TikTok algorithm favors educational + personal narrative
- **Conversion:** Educational content → trust → trial signup (studies show 2-3x higher LTV)
