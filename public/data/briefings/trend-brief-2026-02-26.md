## 🔴 CRITICAL MARKET GAPS IDENTIFIED

### 1. **Game Film Review is UNSOLVED**
**Highest-friction problem in youth basketball coaching right now.**

From r/basketballcoach (July 2025):
> "Reviewing game film. There is no great option if you're coaching AAU or youth basketball."

- **Current workaround:** Coaches use iPhone + iOS Photos app captions (manual, slow, terrible UX)
- **Why Hudl/FastScout fail youth:** Designed for college/pro → expensive, bloated, overkill
- **Why this matters:** Coaches WANT it but have zero good options at youth level

**CourtLab Opening:** Build lightweight film review module → clip extraction + annotation + team sharing
**Content Play:** "Why your coaching film is stuck in 2010 (and how to fix it in 5 mins)"

### 2. **Player Tracking Gap: "Fair Playtime" Anxiety**
**Parents demand transparency. Coaches have zero accountability tools.**

Multiple Reddit posts about apps that track player playtime/substitutions:
- Coaches need to justify rotation decisions
- AAU/youth parents increasingly litigious about playing time
- Currently: Email spreadsheets, mental notes, or expensive Hudl

**CourtLab Opening:** Real-time playtime dashboard + parent-visible transparency + per-game stats
**Positioning:** "The data coach's way to solve 'My kid doesn't get enough minutes'"

## 📊 MARKET TRENDS (Macro Context)

### Smart Facility Tech Explosion
- $18.85B sports tech market → $61.72B by 2030
- Pixellot processed **1.5M games in 2025** (automated video capture)
- GameChanger's AI cameras now rolling out to rec-level baseball/softball
- **Implication for CourtLab:** Video infrastructure becoming standard → our combine timer + analytics tap into this trend

### Content Creator Consolidation
- **SkillShow** filming 300+ events/year for Perfect Game USA, IMG Academy, Under Armour
- PE funds flooding youth sports tech (Drake Star: $52B in H1 2025)
- **KKR-backed PlayOn acquired MaxPreps; Hudl acquired SportContract, Titan Sports; Stack Sports acquired PlayMetrics**
- Trend: Individual club apps → consolidation around mega-platforms
- **What this means:** CourtLab must position as "coaches' tool" not "facility system" to stay lean

### Cost Barrier Increasing
- Avg US sports family spent **$1,016 on primary sport in 2024** (46% increase since 2019)
- Tariffs + inflation → families cutting costs
- Youth watching less basketball (shorter attention spans, YouTube shorts > full games)
- **Opportunity:** Free/freemium tools win in down economy
