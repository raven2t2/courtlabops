## 🚀 FEATURE IDEAS FROM RESEARCH

### Gap 1: "Hudl is too expensive for what I need"
**Feature:** CourtLab Lite — Video + Basic Stats + Player Profiles @ 1/3 Hudl price
- One-click team upload from phone
- Auto-tag key moments (fast break, defensive stop, turnover)
- Player performance card (shooting %, efficiency, consistency trends)
- Compare player A vs B in 10 seconds (not 10 minutes of Hudl navigation)

**Win:** "Cheaper than Hudl, 10x simpler, actually built for coaches not scouts"

---

### Gap 2: "I need proof players are improving for recruiting"
**Feature:** Growth Dashboard — Week-to-week/month-to-month progress graphs
- Shooting accuracy by zone (corner 3, mid-range, paint)
- Ball handling consistency (turnover rate)
- Defensive intensity (steals, blocks per touch)
- Visual comparison cards: "Player X was 42% FG% Week 1, now 58% Week 4 — your recruit IS improving"

**Win:** "Show parents & recruits the TRUTH (CourtLab mission) — measurable growth, not hype"

---

### Gap 3: "Hudl's AI stats are broken"
**Feature:** Verified Stats Mode — Human-verified baseline for accuracy
- Community coaches co-verify games (crowdsourced accuracy)
- Confidence score: "This stat is verified by 5 coaches" vs "AI-generated, unverified"
- Eliminates the frustration: "Hudl stats miss players and mess up fairness"

**Win:** "The ONE app where coaches actually trust the data"

---

### Gap 4: "How do I know who's combine-ready?"
**Feature:** Combine Readiness Scorecard
- CourtLab auto-tracks measurables: shooting arc consistency, footwork efficiency, game-speed decision-making
- Benchmark: "Your player is in 75th percentile for footwork at this age"
- Actionable: "Work on mid-range consistency to unlock elite tier"

**Win:** "Help coaches develop players TO the combine, not just identify them"

---

### Gap 5: "I want analytics without complexity"
**Feature:** Coach Briefing — 30-second daily summary card
- "Team shooting: ↑ from 44% to 49% this week"
- "Your top 3 guards: efficiency trending up"
- "One red flag: 3-point variance too high (38%-62%)"
- "Recommended drill: corner 3 consistency (game tape analysis)"

**Win:** "Analytics that respect a coach's time (not a college analyst's thesis)"
