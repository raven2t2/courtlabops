## 🎯 IDEA #1: "Combine Coach Challenge" — Competitive Activation Funnel (Drive Trial Signups)

### The Problem
34 free users are dormant. Why? They signed up but have no reason to pay. The Combine timer was a download driver, not a value trigger. They need **social proof + competition + urgency** to convert to trial.

### The Move
**Launch a 2-week "Combine Coach Challenge"** on Instagram Reels + TikTok:

**Campaign Concept:**
- **Hook:** "Can your team beat the AU Combine benchmark? (Spoiler: Probably not)"
- **Mechanic:** Coaches film short Combine drills (30 sec max), tag @CourtLab, we score them against benchmarks
- **Prize:** Winner gets featured in "AU's Best Combine Performance" post + free 3-month trial
- **Call-to-action:** Download the free Combine Timer app, track your team's scores, see how you rank

**Why It Works:**
- **Viral loop:** Coaches share videos (free PR) → Their players/parents see app → Join for free → Score appears in-app → Unlock full benchmark comparison (paywall gate)
- **Urgency:** 2-week window (Feb 27 - Mar 13) → FOMO on rankings
- **Competitive angle:** Australian coaches will naturally compare themselves to other clubs. You're just giving them a public scoreboard.
- **Positioning:** Contrasts perfectly with Tagup/Synergy (film-focused). We're data-focused, objective, fun.
- **Activation of 34 dormant users:** They can participate too → Invite their clubs → Network effect

**Tactical Execution (Today — Friday)**

**1. Creative Assets (120 mins)**
- **Video hook (15 sec):** Michael on bench with stopwatch: "My club thought they could beat the AU average. Here's what happened..." [Shows benchmark vs. their time, benchmark wins]
- **Reels template (30 sec):** Animated benchmark overlay (green if beating AU avg, red if not) with coach names/clubs
- **Static posts (3x):** Leaderboard mockup, highlight winner bio, rules

**2. Setup (60 mins)**
- Create Instagram "Combine Challenge" hashtag guide
- Build simple scoring rubric (accuracy, consistency, speed)
- Pre-record 2-3 demo videos yourself (to seed the pool + show it's easy)
- Design winner announcement template

**3. Launch (60 mins)**
- Post hook on @CourtLab Instagram (Friday 5 PM) + TikTok (Friday 6 PM)
- Tag 10 Australian basketball coaches/clubs with DM: "Your team in this? [Video link]"
- Pin post to stories for 48 hours

**4. Day 2 Activation (Monday)**

- Email to all affiliate club contacts (from CRM): "2-week challenge — FREE Combine Timer, compete for prizes"

**Timeline to Trial:**

- Day 8-14: "See your full team's benchmark data" paywall → Trial conversion

## 🎯 IDEA #3: "Combine Timer + Benchmark Bundle" — Affiliate Partnership Incentive Structure (Sticky Revenue Model)

### The Problem
ASA campaign launches Monday with 5 keywords. Will generate clicks, but conversion is still 0%. And even if someone signs up for trial, we have no affiliate revenue model. Coaches won't pay for individual subscriptions.

### The Move
**Create an affiliate partnership tier** that coaches can sell to their clubs (commission structure):

**Three-Tier Model:**
```
TIER 1: COMBINE TIMER (Free)
- Stopwatch + score tracker for one player
- No benchmarks, no team data
- Free forever (lead magnet)

TIER 2: TEAM COMBO ($49/month per club)
- Track full roster (25 players max)
- See team vs. AU benchmarks
- Affiliate coach gets 30% ($14.70/month per club)
- Example: 5 clubs = $73.50/month passive income

TIER 3: COACHING SUITE ($199/month per club)
- Unlimited players
- Advanced recruitment features
- Affiliate coach gets 25% ($49.75/month per club)
- Example: 2 clubs = $99.50/month passive income

AFFILIATE PLAY:
- Coach recruits their own clubs (they already know 5-10)
- Coach pitches CourtLab to club director at board meeting: "Objective talent evaluation. No fluff."
- Coach keeps 25-30% commission monthly (recurring)
- CourtLab keeps 70-75%, scales via affiliate network (not paid ads)
```

**Why It Works:**
- **Coaches are your sales team:** They have credibility with other coaches. One coach's recommendation = gold.
- **Recurring revenue:** Monthly commissions create lock-in (coach stays invested in your success).
- **CAC reduction:** Affiliate-driven signups cost ZERO ad spend. Just commission.
- **Network effects:** 16 club contacts become 16 salespeople. Each recruits 3-5 more. Exponential growth.
- **Competitive moat:** Tagup/Synergy can't create this fast. They're enterprise-focused, you're grassroots-focused.
- **Positioning:** "We scale through coach advocacy, not paid ads" = authentic narrative for Series B.
