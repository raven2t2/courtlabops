## IDEA #2: Recruiting Myth-Busting Series (Social Velocity)
### Category: Organic Social + Coach Authority | Priority: HIGH

**Thesis:** Own the "objective basketball data" narrative on Instagram/Twitter. Launch 3-post daily rotation debunking recruiting/coaching myths. Positions CourtLab as the coach education platform (not sales tool). Drives organic traffic to site for recruiting coaches + parents.

**Content Angles (3 pillars — rotate M/W/F and Tue/Thu/Sat):**

**Pillar A: "Highlight Reel vs. Reality"**
- **Format:** Reels/Shorts (30-45 sec) + static quote on Twitter
- **Hook:** "This player's Instagram reel: 40-point highlight. CourtLab data: 22% FG%, 8 turnovers. Which matters?"
- **Message:** Subjective vs. objective. Parents/coaches get hype, not truth.
- **CTA (Soft):** "What's your recruit missing? Objective data changes everything."

**Pillar B: "Coach Secret: The Data"**
- **Format:** Carousel (Instagram) showing coach quote + stat insight
- **Example:** "Best coach on my staff said 'I can tell who's committed by their body language.' CourtLab data: 60% of committed players never show up to extra workouts. Guess what actually matters?"
- **Message:** Intuition + data = unstoppable. Data alone = credible.
- **CTA (Soft):** "Coaches: Track what you see. Prove what you believe."

**Pillar C: "Parent ROI Check"**
- **Format:** Short story-format post (Twitter thread or Carousel)
- **Example:** "Parent spent $12k AAU last year. Coach said 'great season.' Stats say: 15 PPG → 14 PPG, same usage. Is $12k working? Here's how to know for sure."
- **Message:** Parents deserve proof, not hope.
- **CTA (Soft):** "Download: Free Parent ROI Checklist"

**Execution (This Week):**
- **Mon/Wed/Fri:** Pillar A (Highlight vs. Reality)
- **Tue/Thu/Sat:** Pillar B (Coach Secret)
- **Sun/Thu:** Pillar C (Parent ROI)
- **Twitter:** Repost text versions daily (higher velocity platform)
- **Hashtags:** #CoachLife #BasketballData #RecruitingTruth #CourtLab

**Why It Works:**
- **No paid spend:** Organic reach to coaches/parents searching these questions
- **High-intent audience:** People searching "recruiting myth" or "coach data" = CourtLab buyers
- **SEO flywheel:** Series builds thematic authority on Google (recruit bias, coach intuition, AAU ROI)
- **Social proof:** Comments from coaches validating data-driven approach = credibility

**Expected Outcome:** 100-200 organic impressions daily + 5-10 site clicks by Friday + content library for next 8 weeks
