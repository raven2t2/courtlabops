## ⚡ EXECUTION-READY IDEA #2: "AU Basketball Recruiting Standards" Benchmark Report (Content + Lead Gen)

### The Problem
Youth basketball coaches in Australia have **NO public benchmarks** for player evaluation. Are 25 cm vertical jump good? Is 95% free throw good? How do my players stack vs. state average?

Series B narrative: "We own the data. We're the source of truth."

### The Move
**Publish a FREE public "AU Basketball Recruiting Benchmark Report"** with:
- Top 10 combine metrics for Guards, Forwards, Centers (AU averages)
- Regional breakdown (SA vs. VIC vs. NSW)
- Recruiting tiers (Elite, High, Development, Entry-level)
- "Unlock full data" CTA → trial signup

This becomes:
1. **SEO asset** — "Basketball recruiting standards Australia" ranks for trial traffic
2. **Lead magnet** — Coaches download report → email captured → nurture sequence
3. **PR hook** — "CourtLab releases first AU basketball benchmark report"
4. **Series B proof** — Data asset proving market size + coach demand

### Implementation (2 hours execution)

**1. Data Pull (30 mins)**
- Query CourtLab backend: Aggregate all Combine results by position
- Calculate: Mean, median, percentiles for key metrics (vert jump, speed, efficiency)
- Group by region (SA, VIC, NSW) + age group (U12, U14, U16, U18)
- Create CSV export with 200+ data points

**Key Metrics:**
- Vertical Jump (cm)
- Court Speed (time)
- Shooting Efficiency (%)
- Ball Handling (dribble speed)
- Agility (lateral movement time)
- Game IQ (positioning, passing accuracy)

**2. Report Creation (1 hour)**
- **Format:** 1-page PDF benchmark chart (print-ready)
- **Design:** Clean, branded (CourtLab colors), easy to scan
- **Content:**
  - Headline: "AU Basketball Recruiting Standards 2026 — Where Does Your Team Rank?"
  - Chart 1: By position (Guards/Forwards/Centers)
  - Chart 2: By region (SA/VIC/NSW/QLD)
  - Chart 3: Age group breakdown (U14 vs U16 vs U18)
  - Footer: "Want full player comparison data? Start your trial →"

**3. Deployment (30 mins)**
- **Host:** Upload to courtlabops.vercel.app `/reports/benchmark-2026.pdf`
- **Lead Magnet:** Add to homepage → "Download AU Basketball Recruiting Standards"
- **Email:** One-off message to existing 34 users + warm contacts
- **LinkedIn:** Michael posts "Just released AU basketball benchmarks. Here's what we found..."
- **Social:** TikTok/Instagram carousel (5 posts, 1 per day, each highlighting 1 metric)
